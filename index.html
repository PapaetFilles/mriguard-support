MRI Guard

Protect your devices from MRI

iPhoneApple Watch10 languages

Your iPhone and Apple Watch are among your most important and most expensive companions — photos, chats, cards, half your life. And of all places, their most dangerous opponent waits at your workplace: an MRI scanner's magnet is never off. One step too far, and the field can wipe or damage your device — or tear it from your hand in an instant. We all know to be careful — and still it happens: an emergency, stress, one moment of distraction — and a device that holds so much is done for.

Made for everyone who regularly deals with MRI: radiographers and MRI technologists, radiologists, other physicians and nurses in hospitals and practices, service engineers and researchers — and patients with a smartphone in their pocket.

With MRI Guard, your devices protect themselves: the app reads the magnetic field around them and sounds a loud, unmistakable alarm as it climbs toward the danger zone — your moment to stop, check, and step back.

What it does

  • Live magnetic-field reading with a clear colour scale (green → red)
  • Adjustable alarm threshold — default at the 0.5 mT / 5-gauss controlled-area line
  • Loud alarm and vibration when the field crosses your threshold
  • Keeps monitoring in the background, even with the screen locked
  • Optional Smart Alarm to reduce false alarms from a magnet brought to a resting device
  • Units in microtesla (µT), millitesla (mT) or gauss (G)
  • Automatic start or arrival reminder at your saved workplace (iPhone), plus Shortcuts and Siri
  • Pauses itself while the device is charging — induction chargers create strong fields of their own — and resumes the moment you unplug
  • Warns you clearly when iOS Low Power Mode is on, which can delay background monitoring

iPhone & Apple Watch

The iPhone is the heart of MRI Guard — and where the app is at its best: once started, it keeps measuring while it sits in your pocket, and it can start by itself when you arrive at work.

With MRI Guard on the Apple Watch, you carry the guard right on your wrist: the watch measures on its own, alarms on its own, and shows you the field at a glance. Start it with a tap on the watch (exactly one with a watch-face complication) or on your iPhone — or let it start along automatically whenever monitoring starts on the iPhone. Alarms from the iPhone appear on your wrist as well. watchOS demands a few compromises of continuously measuring apps — the app keeps itself in the foreground of the watch, uses somewhat more battery than on the iPhone, and only the iPhone may react to your arrival at work. We call that what it is: that's why the watch app carries a Beta label.

Arriving at work — good to know

Save your workplace once and automatic start arms monitoring hands-free the moment you arrive — a notification confirms it. Prefer to decide yourself? Switch to the arrival reminder, where one tap on the notification starts monitoring instead. Either way it stops by itself when you leave. This uses Apple's geofencing; for reliable detection allow Always location access with Precise Location on, keep Wi-Fi enabled (iOS uses it to recognise places, even without a connection), and avoid Low Power Mode. iOS typically reports an arrival one to three minutes after you reach a saved place — and opening the app always starts monitoring right away. For large buildings you can increase a place's detection radius in the app.

Private by design

No account. No sign-in. Your saved locations stay on your device and are never uploaded. The app can share anonymous, aggregate usage statistics to help improve it — you can turn that off anytime in Settings. See our Privacy Policy.

Please read. MRI Guard is an awareness aid — not a certified safety device or medical instrument. The magnetic sensor in a phone or watch is uncalibrated and saturates at a few millitesla, so it cannot measure strong fields accurately and may not react in every situation. Never rely on it for safety-critical decisions. Always follow your site's MR safety rules and use approved measuring equipment. You are responsible for your own safety and your devices.
